 ABOUT ME: Corina Kaiser


Hello! My name is Corina Kaiser. I am a NYC-based artist, athlete, academic, and activist.


I'm now studying for a MS in Mathematical Sciences at NYU Tandon in Brooklyn, NY with the expected graduation date of May 2026. I received bachelor's degrees in Mathematics, Applied German, and Philosophy from Penn State University, then began graduate school after two years of teaching math and science to adult learners via SUNY.


In addition to my academic pursits, I have been playing rugby for over a decade. After playing at Penn State, I was honored to be drafted for and play in the inaugural season of the USA's first ever professional women's rugby league: Women's Elite Rugby (WER). I played the 2025 season with the New York Exiles and plan to continue playing with them in Spring 2026.


I am also a multidisciplinary artist who likes to experiment with painting, collage, film photography, and sewing/ clothing design.


After graduating from NYU Tandon, I am looking to enter into a career where I can utilize my strong analytical skills, creative flair, and love of working with a team.
