ABOUT ME: Corina Kaiser

Hello! My name is Corina Kaiser. I am an NYC-based researcher, athlete, and artist.


Check out my Github here.


I completed my MS in Mathematical Sciences at NYU Tandon (May 2025) after earning bachelor's degrees in Mathematics, Applied German, and Philosophy from Penn State University. My research interests sit at the intersection of machine learning and foundational theory, with a current focus on mechanistic interpretability and representation engineering. Among other projects, I have built a multi-agent dialogue system,
brain.vat, to allow two GPT-2-based models to interact autonomously.


I've played rugby for over a decade, including five years at Penn State. I was then drafted to the inaugural season of Women's Elite Rugby (WER), the first professional women's rugby league in the US, and played the 2025 season with New York Exiles.


I am also a multidisciplinary artist who works across painting, collage, film photography, and sewing/clothing design. On the Photography page, you can find some examples of my work.


I am actively seeking research-oriented roles in machine learning and AI.
