About

Research, systems, and strange interfaces

I’m Corina Kaiser, an NYC-based research engineer with an MS in Mathematical Sciences from NYU Tandon. I build AI systems and research tools that make model behavior inspectable, usable, and operational—from multi-agent memory and real-time applications to representation-engineering experiments and physical interfaces.

My practice sits between mathematical investigation and product engineering. I care about what a system does, how we can measure it, and what it takes to put it in someone’s hands responsibly. That has led me to work across retrieval and agent memory, full-stack collaboration tools, interactive data visualization, model-routing research, and embedded interfaces.

I began this path through experimental dialogue systems and still value the unusual, generative questions that brought me there. brain.vat ↗ remains an early example: a multi-agent dialogue experiment built around autonomous GPT-2 interaction.
