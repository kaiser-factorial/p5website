ABOUT ME: Corina Kaiser


Hello! My name is Corina Kaiser. I am an NYC-based academic, athlete, and artist.


Check out my Github here.


I'm completing my MS in Mathematical Sciences at NYU Tandon (expected grad May 2026) after earning bachelor's degrees in Mathematics, Applied German, and Philosophy from Penn State University. My current research focuses on the conditions under which complex behavioral patterns may emerge from simpler models. To explore this interest, I have built a multi-agent dialogue system brain.vat to allow two GPT2-based models to interact autonomously.


I've played rugby for over a decade, including five years at Penn State. I was then drafted to the inaugural season of Women's Elite Rugby (WER), the first professional women's rugby league in the US, and played the 2025 season with New York Exiles.


I am also a multidisciplinary artist who works across painting, collage, film photography, and sewing/ clothing design. On the Photography page, you can find some examples of my work.


After graduating from NYU Tandon, I am looking to work somewhere that will allow me to learn continuously and apply my curiosity in a research-oriented context.
