Rate My Professor analysis

An academic analysis of nearly 90,000 student evaluations, using regression, PCA, and permutation testing to investigate patterns in ratings and qualitative descriptors.

Project summary

This project explored relationships among student-assigned ratings, qualitative tags, discipline, and other available evaluation variables. The analysis used Weighted Least Squares, logistic regression, PCA, and permutation testing to frame questions around patterns and association.
