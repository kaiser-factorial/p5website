Data Science Projects

Hypothesis Testing of Movie Ratings Data

Course project for DS-GA 1001: Internship in Data Science (Fall 2025).

This report contains answers determined by analyzing a dataset which includes ratings data of 400 movies from 1097 research participants. Aside from viewers’ movie ratings from 0 to 4, the survey also gathered information regarding self-assessments on sensation seeking behaviors, responses to personality questions, self-reported movie experience ratings, gender identity, whether responders were only children, and whether responders believed that movies are best enjoyed alone. Analysis is based upon a significance level of 𝛼 = 0.005.

Rate My Professor Data Analysis and Modeling

Capstone report for DS-GA 1001 (Fall 2025).

This report combines hypothesis testing and machine learning to investigate patterns in student evaluations across almost 90k professor records.
