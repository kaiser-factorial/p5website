Catch-Fall

Overview: Move your head left and right to catch the yellow falling items and avoid red ones. This project utilizes ml5.js facial tracking for a unique hands-free interaction style.

In Catch-Fall, you control a blue character by moving your head. The tracking focuses on your forehead to determine movement. Catch yellow pieces to level up, but be careful—the speed and frequency increase quickly!
