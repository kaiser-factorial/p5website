DRAWING CONTROLS

This sketchpad is controlled by your web-cam.

  • Pinch THUMB and POINTER finger to draw.
  • Press UP/DOWN to change stroke width.
  • Press SPACEBAR to CLEAR canvas.
  • Press ENTER to SAVE canvas.

COLOR KEYS

SPEAK COLOR NAME TO CHANGE STROKE COLOR!

Or, press key(s) to change stroke color:

R = RED   O = ORANGE   Y = YELLOW

G = GREEN   B = BLUE   P = PURPLE

0 = BLACK

Press "E" to toggle Eraser.
