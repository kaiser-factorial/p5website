Sketchpad

HOLD MOUSE to draw.

Or, DOUBLE CLICK to toggle Continuous Draw mode on/off.

Press UP/DOWN arrow keys to increase/decrease stroke width.

Press "E" to toggle Eraser.

Press SPACEBAR to clear canvas.

Press keys to change stroke color:

R = RED    O = ORANGE    Y = YELLOW G = GREEN   B = BLUE   P = PURPLE 0 = BLACK
